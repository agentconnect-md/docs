The WeChat integration connects **your personal WeChat account** to an agent. After you scan a QR code in the Console, the agent appears as a private contact under WeChat's ClawBot plugin. It can't join groups, and no one else can message it. For a bot your whole team can talk to, use [WeCom](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/wecom).

WeChat works on both AgentConnect Cloud and self-hosted deployments.

<Callout>
  ClawBot is an experimental feature in WeChat and may change or stop working on WeChat's side.
</Callout>

Because the daemon connects outbound to WeChat, you don't need a public callback URL or any manual credentials. Scanning the QR code completes the setup.

## Connect it

1. On your agent's page, open **Integrations → Add integration → WeChat** and click **Show QR code**.
2. Scan the code with WeChat on your phone. If you're already on that phone, tap **On this phone already? Open in WeChat** instead. The code expires after about two minutes; refresh it if needed.

   <Image src={imgQr} alt="The WeChat step of Add integration, showing a QR code to scan" width={640} />

3. Tap **Confirm** in WeChat. If WeChat displays a number on your phone, enter it in the Console.
4. Once the Console shows **Connected to WeChat user**, open WeChat, find the bot under ClawBot, and send it a message.

Whoever scans the QR code becomes the bot's only chat partner. Scan it with your own WeChat and don't share the code.

## Use it

- **Chat**: Message the bot directly. The chat runs as one ongoing session with the agent, and its sessions in the Console are visible only to you.
- **Replies**: WeChat displays text verbatim, so replies arrive as plain text with Markdown stripped. Each message arrives complete, a paragraph or more at a time, and long answers are split across several messages in order. Nothing appears while the agent works before its first reply, and WeChat can take several seconds to deliver a message to the agent.
- **Files and images**: Send the bot an image, file, or video. Voice messages arrive as WeChat's built-in transcriptions. The agent can't send images or files back yet.
- **Questions**: WeChat doesn't support buttons or cards, so questions that require picking an option can't be asked in chat. The chat gets a notice instead.
- **Approvals**: When a tool call needs approval, an Agent editor handles it in the Console, and the chat gets a notice.

See [In-conversation commands](/connect-automate/integrations-overview#in-conversation-commands) for stopping, cancelling, queuing, and changing supported session settings.

## Notes

- If WeChat logs the bot out, it stops answering. Open **Integrations → Messaging apps → WeChat → Reconnect** and scan again with the same WeChat account. Your integration is kept; the current conversation closes, and your next message starts a new one.
- If the bot moves to another daemon, messages sent during the move are lost.
- Each WeChat bot binds to one agent and one person. Deleting the integration also deletes the bot. To connect again, scan a new QR code.
- When you leave the organization, the WeChat bots you connected are deleted along with your membership.

## Self-hosting

WeChat is disabled by default on self-hosted deployments. Enable it with `features.wechat` in the Helm chart. On Docker Compose, add `wechat` to `FEATURE_FLAGS` in [`compose.env`](/self-hosting/docker-compose/configuration#compose-environment) and recreate `web`. Until the flag is on, WeChat won't appear in the integration picker.

Connect agents to [Slack](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/slack), [Microsoft Teams](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/msteams), [Google Chat](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/googlechat), [Discord](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/discord), [Telegram](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/telegram), [Lark / Feishu](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/lark-feishu), [DingTalk](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/dingtalk), [WeCom](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/wecom), or [WeChat](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/wechat). Self-hosted deployments can also connect [QQ](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/qq). Each integration binds an agent to a bot and a set of conversations.

<Image src={imgAddIntegration} alt="Choose a chat platform for an agent" width={640} />

On AgentConnect Cloud, Slack uses the built-in **Add to Slack** flow. Lark / Feishu also offers a recommended one-click setup, and Microsoft Teams connects through an administrator sign-in. Google Chat, Discord, Telegram, DingTalk, WeCom, and QQ require credentials from their own platforms, while WeChat connects by scanning a QR code.

See [Bots](/connect-automate/chat-platforms/bots) for reusable bot identities and shared-bot routing. For a multi-agent example, see [One Slack app with different agents by channel](/how-to-guides/one-slack-app-across-channels).

## Manage conversations

Once a bot discovers a channel, group, or direct message, that conversation appears on the agent's integration card. Channels and groups can respond to mentions only, to all messages, or stay Off. Each one-to-one direct message has its own **On / Off** toggle for that agent: it starts On for agents visible to Everyone, and Off for [restricted agents](/operate-govern/permissions-overview/team-visibility#what-a-restricted-agent-changes).

Turning a conversation **Off** mutes inbound triggers without removing the bot or blocking outbound work such as schedules and agent handoffs. To remove the bot from a conversation, manage its membership on the chat platform.

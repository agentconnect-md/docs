WeCom is off by default. On a self-hosted deployment, turn it on with `features.wecom` in the Helm chart; on Docker Compose, add `wecom` to `FEATURE_FLAGS` in [`compose.env`](/self-hosting/docker-compose/configuration#compose-environment) and recreate `web`. Until the flag is on, WeCom does not appear in the integration picker. AgentConnect Cloud does not offer it yet.

Each organization connects a smart bot from its own WeCom. The daemon connects outbound through the smart bot's long connection, so no public callback URL is required. Privately deployed WeCom is not supported.

## Create the smart bot

In the [WeCom admin console](https://work.weixin.qq.com/wework_admin/frame#/aiHelper/create), signed in as an admin of your organization:

1. Fill in the bot's name, introduction, and avatar with your agent's. WeCom shows them in chats, and AgentConnect cannot set them for you. Then choose **API 模式** (API mode).

   <Image src={imgCreateBot} alt="Create a smart bot: a name, an introduction, and API mode selected" width={640} />

2. Under **API 配置** (API configuration), keep **连接方式** (connection method) on **使用长连接** (long connection).

   <Image src={imgApiConfig} alt="API configuration with long connection selected, above the Bot ID and Secret" width={640} />

3. Under **使用模式** (usage mode), change **使用方式** from the default **仅个人使用** (personal use only) to **多人使用** (multiple people), and choose under **可使用成员** the members who may use the bot.

   <Image src={imgUsageMode} alt="Usage mode set to multiple people, with the members who may use the bot" width={640} />

4. Click **保存** (Save), then copy the **Bot ID** from **API 配置** and click **点击获取** to get the **Secret**.

A smart bot allows one long connection at a time. Give AgentConnect a bot nothing else connects to: a second program on the same bot disconnects the first.

## Connect it

On your agent, open **Integrations → Add integration → WeCom**, paste the Bot ID and Secret, and click **Connect**. AgentConnect checks the pair with WeCom before saving it; a bot whose connection method is not long connection is refused with what to change.

<Image src={imgIntegration} alt="Add a WeCom integration with a BotID and Secret" width={640} />

## Use it

- **Direct chats** — message the bot directly. A direct chat is one ongoing session with the agent.
- **Groups** — add the bot to a group and **@-mention** it. WeCom delivers a group message only when it mentions the bot. A WeCom group has no threads, so everyone in the group shares one conversation with the agent, and the group's [**Session mode**](/connect-automate/integrations-overview#session-mode) on the agent's Integrations card decides how the agent organizes the work in it.
- **Replies** — the answer streams in as the agent writes it, formatted as Markdown. A long answer continues in follow-up messages, because WeCom ends a streamed message after ten minutes and caps its size.
- **Files and images** — send the bot an image, file, or video in a direct chat; in a group, attach images to the message that mentions the bot, or quote a file while mentioning it. A voice message arrives as WeCom's own transcription. An agent can share images (PNG, JPEG, or GIF, up to 10 MB) and other files (up to 20 MB) from its workspace back.
- **Questions** — when the agent asks you to choose one option, it sends a card and you answer with a tap. A question that does not fit a card arrives as a numbered list instead: quote that message and reply with the number, and in a group also @-mention the bot.
- **Approvals** — WeCom has no approval card. When a tool call needs approval, an Agent editor answers it in the console, and the conversation gets a notice.

See [In-conversation commands](/connect-automate/integrations-overview#in-conversation-commands) for stopping, cancelling, queuing, and changing supported session settings.

## Notes

- WeCom gives a smart bot no group or member names, so the Console lists conversations as `WeCom group · <tag>` and people as `WeCom member · <tag>`, or by account ID where WeCom sends a readable one.
- WeCom offers a smart bot no reaction or typing indicator, so the first sign of work is the streamed reply itself.
- WeCom limits a bot to 30 messages a minute and 1,000 an hour in each conversation. Near the limit the stream refreshes less often; if WeCom refuses part of an answer, the full text stays in the session.
- The daemon fetches an attachment when it arrives and keeps it for 24 hours. After that, or after the daemon restarts, send the file again.
- A group responds to mentions or not at all, and AgentConnect cannot remove the bot from a group. Remove it in WeCom.
- One WeCom bot binds to one agent. Deleting the integration frees the bot for reuse (**An unused WeCom bot** next time); it does not delete the smart bot from WeCom.
- Replace the Secret under **Integrations → Messaging apps → WeCom → Replace secret**. AgentConnect cannot test the new Secret without disconnecting the running bot, so if the bot stops responding afterwards, copy the Secret from WeCom again.
